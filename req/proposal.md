# Clinic Management System — Project Proposal


## 1. What We're Building

A single web-based system for your clinic that replaces manual registers with:

- Patient registration & search
- Doctor slip / token generation for each patient visit
- Billing & receipts for Laboratory, X-Ray, ECG, ECO (Echo) and Ultrasound
- Operation Theater booking, room/bed management, and final billing
- A management dashboard showing daily/weekly/monthly cash, doctor performance, and expenses
- Doctor, employee, and price-list management screens — **Management adds doctors and every test/price directly inside the system whenever needed; nothing is fixed in advance or needs to be handed over to us**
- Secure logins for staff (Operator) and clinic owner/admin (Management)

## 7. Estimated Timeline — 2 Weeks

The full 300 hours of work will be delivered in **2 weeks (14 days)** instead of the usual 10–12 weeks.

**Day-by-day plan:**

| Days | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Setup, screen design, logins, patient registration & search |
| 3–5 | Doctor registration, doctor slip/token, and all 5 billing counters (Lab, X-Ray, ECG, ECO, Ultrasound) built in parallel |
| 6–9 | Operation Theater module, price/test management, expenses & salaries |
| 10–12 | Management dashboard, speed optimization, and full testing |
| 13–14 | Fixes from testing, staff training, and handover |

**⚠️ What a 2-week delivery requires (please note):**

Compressing ~3 months of work into 2 weeks doesn't reduce the work itself — it means several people build different parts at the same time instead of one developer going step by step. For this to work smoothly:

- A **small team (3–4 developers)** works in parallel, not a single developer — the total cost (PKR 60,000) and total hours (300) stay the same either way.
- Doctors, tests, and prices do **not** need to be prepared in advance — those screens are part of the build, and Management enters real data directly into the system once it's live.
- **Same-day feedback** is needed at each review point (Day 5, Day 9, Day 12) — a few days' delay on your end pushes the final date by the same amount.
- **Access to your cloud server** should be ready before Day 10, so deployment/testing on the real environment isn't delayed.
- Testing time is tighter than a normal-paced project, so the first week of real clinic use should be watched closely for minor issues (covered under the free maintenance month).
- Any scope change mid-way (a new field, a new report) is treated as a new feature request (Section 5), not squeezed into the 2 weeks.
